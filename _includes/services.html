Find the right level of support for your manuscript

Advanced

Substantive editing

Substantive editing is an advanced level of editing. I will shape your results into a clear scientific story, refining the structure, argument, figures, and language so your research reads as clearly as it deserves.

Right for you if:
  • You’re submitting a thesis, manuscript, or grant proposal, and want the best chance of success
  • Your results are solid, but the manuscript still feels unclear or difficult to structure.
  • You want your paper to tell a compelling scientific story, instead of a collection of disconnected results.
  • You need a fresh, professional perspective after spending too long inside the same draft.

Basic

Copyediting

Copyediting is a basic level of editing. I will smooth out awkward phrasing, repetition, inconsistencies, and language errors to make your manuscript clearer and easier to read.

Right for you if:
  • Your thesis, manuscript, or grant proposal is nearly there and only needs final refinements before submission.
  • Your ideas and results are well organized but need polishing for clarity and flow.
  • You want the language to remain concise, coherent, and consistent throughout the manuscript.
  • You need the document formatted according to journal or university guidelines.

Manuscript evaluation

A manuscript evaluation is a detailed editorial review of your manuscript. I will read through the draft carefully and provide actionable feedback on the scientific narrative, structure, clarity, language, and overall flow of the paper.

Right for you if:
  • You want to better understand the strengths and weaknesses of your draft.
  • You want detailed feedback before revising the manuscript yourself with confidence.
  • You need a fresh pair of eyes to review the manuscript in depth.
  • You want to maximize the chances of success for your manuscript or grant proposal.

Figure Design

Scientific figures are often the first thing readers look at. Clear, thoughtful visuals can make complex results easier to understand, strengthen your scientific story, and increase the overall impact of your work. Right for you if:
