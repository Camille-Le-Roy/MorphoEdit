About Me

Hello! I’m Camille. I help researchers and data professionals transform complex, high-variability data into clear, high-impact narratives. With a Ph.D. in evolutionary biology and biophysics, I bring over 8 years of experience in the high-level publication lifecycle.

I have authored several first-author publications in journals like Science and Nature Communications, and I serve as a peer reviewer for over 12 academic journals. My mission with Morpho Editing is to provide the coaching and editing support necessary to navigate the complexities of academic publishing.
